# Sizer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Sizer is a menu bar utility for macOS that resizes and repositions windows with keyboard shortcuts.

## What Sizer collects

Nothing. Sizer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data.

- **No accounts.** Sizer has no sign-in and no user accounts.
- **No network access.** Sizer never connects to the internet. It makes no network requests of any kind, so nothing leaves your Mac.
- **No analytics or tracking.** Sizer contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting frameworks.
- **No third parties.** Because no data is collected, none is shared with anyone.

## What Sizer stores on your Mac

Your preferences (keyboard shortcuts, animation speed, whether Sizer is enabled, and whether it launches at login) are saved locally using the standard macOS preferences system. They stay on your Mac and are removed when you delete the app's container.

## Accessibility permission

Sizer asks for Accessibility access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. macOS requires this permission for an app to move and resize the windows of other apps and to respond to global keyboard shortcuts. Sizer uses it only for that purpose. It does not read the contents of your windows, record keystrokes, or capture your screen. You can revoke the permission at any time in System Settings.

## Children

Sizer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
