Sizer snaps the window you are working in to exactly where you want it, with a keystroke. Halves, quarters, centered, or full screen: press the shortcut and the window moves. No dragging edges, no hunting for the green button.

It lives in your menu bar, stays out of your Dock, and gets out of the way.

SNAP WITH SHORTCUTS
• Left, right, top, or bottom half
• Any of the four quarters
• Maximize to fill the screen
• Center at 75% or 50% of the screen

The default shortcuts use Control-Option-Command and Control-Option-Shift with the arrow keys, so they stay clear of the shortcuts in the apps you already use.

TIDY A WHOLE APP AT ONCE
• Auto Arrange Columns puts every window of the front app side by side
• Auto Arrange 2 Rows lays them out in a two-row grid
• Maximize All Windows fills the screen with each of them

MADE FOR MORE THAN ONE DISPLAY
• Send the front window to the next or previous display, and it keeps its size
• Every snap uses the visible area of the display the window is already on, so nothing ends up behind the Dock or the menu bar

MAKE IT YOURS
• Change any shortcut: click it, press the new keys, done
• Reset to the defaults at any time
• Choose how quickly windows animate, or turn animation off
• Pause Sizer from the menu bar without quitting it
• Launch at login

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sizer never connects to the internet, has no account, and collects nothing. Your preferences stay on your Mac.

Sizer needs Accessibility permission to move and resize windows, as every window manager on macOS does. It asks the first time you launch it, and you can grant access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
